`@choros` in Slack is an agent, not just a task-filer. Mention it in a channel or DM it, describe what you want, and it acts.

![The Choros Slack bot spinning up a workspace and reporting back in-thread](/images/slack-bot.png)

## What It Can Do

- **Create, update, search, and triage tasks** from the conversation it's standing in, pulling assignees, statuses, and priorities from your team's Linear/Choros setup
- **Spin up a cloud workspace and launch a coding agent** to actually do the work. Code-change requests ("bump our Sentry SDK to v9 across all apps") default to spawning a workspace and agent rather than filing a ticket
- **Read the thread or channel history** on its own to gather context, no copy-pasting prior messages
- **Search the web with citations** when a request needs current information
- **Unfurl Choros task and workspace links** with rich previews

## Setup

1. In the dashboard, go to **Integrations → Slack**
2. Click **Connect** to install the Choros Slack app (OAuth)
3. Mention `@choros` in any channel, or DM the bot, and describe what you want done

## Good First Prompts

```text
@choros file a bug: the export button 500s on reports over 10k rows.
Assign to whoever owns reporting.
```

```text
@choros bump our Sentry SDK to v9 across all apps and open a PR.
```

The second one creates a workspace, runs an agent, and reports back in-thread.
